# Food-Delivery-Analysis-Power-BI
Food Delivery Analysis & DashboardAn end-to-end business intelligence study utilizing synthetic dataset generation, Power Query ETL pipelines, advanced DAX modeling, and interactive Power BI dashboards.  Executive SummaryThis project presents a comprehensive analytics solution for a multi-city food delivery service. The objective was to transform raw transactional data into actionable operational and financial insights—evaluating order volumes, customer satisfaction, delivery efficiency, and regional market distribution. Technical Architecture & ToolsDataset Generation (ChatGPT): Prompt-engineered raw food delivery ordering logs simulating real-world order IDs, order dates, cities, delivery times, customer ratings, and restaurant categories.  Data Preparation (Power Query): Automated ETL pipeline to cleanse data types, column names, missing attributes, purge duplicates, and standardize casing.  Analytical Modeling (Power BI & DAX): Built custom DAX measures for real-time executive dashboard decision-making and period-over-period tracking.  Workflow & Data PipelineDataset Generation: Simulated transactional logs containing ordering details across various food categories and regions.  Power Query Pipeline: Imported data into Power BI Desktop, cleansed null values, transformed data types, and purged duplicate records.  Schema Optimization: Structured dimensions and fact relationships for Cities, Categories, and Restaurants to prepare for DAX calculations.  Interactive Dashboard Development: Built both Light and Dark mode user interfaces featuring cross-filtering slicers and drill-down capabilities. Key Business InsightsGeographic Distribution: Kolkata leads total order share with 17.65% (9 orders), followed by Chennai at 15.69% (8 orders) and Bengaluru at 13.73% (7 orders).  Fulfillment Speeds: Cumulative delivery time duration is highest in Kolkata (423 mins) and Chennai (399 mins), highlighting primary operational bottlenecks in those hubs.  Category Performance: Slicers track performance across major categories including Street Food, Fast Food, Continental, North Indian, Desserts & Bakery, Biryani, South Indian, Beverages, and Pizza.
